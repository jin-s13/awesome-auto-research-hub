深度分析

创新点

  • Athanor, an open agent-native system for autonomous post-training research (AutoPostTrain) on real GPUs
  • Gives agents full research autonomy, low-friction engineering tools, and efficient starter code, while operating in a sandbox without external teacher models
  • Provides research infrastructure including frozen evaluation, managed jobs, decontaminated data, a persistent research record, and a held-out suite hidden from agents
  • First demonstration of a team of frontier agents autonomously post-training a raw base model into a cross-domain generalist model that matches or surpasses the official instruct version on average

方法

Athanor was built as an open agent-native system for autonomous post-training research. In a 104-hour campaign, four Claude Opus 5.5 agents, each on an eight-GPU node and given no research advice, post-trained Qwen3-4B-Base using public data. The agents read datasets and evaluation results, built training data, wrote and modified training algorithms, tuned hyperparameters, and analyzed results to decide next steps, while Athanor supplied frozen evaluation, managed jobs, decontaminated data, a persistent research record, and an unseen held-out suite.

关键结果

The agents improved Qwen3-4B-Base from 15.9 to 67.3 on a six-task development suite and from 9.2 to 48.2 on a held-out suite, compared with 66.5 and 47.9 for the official Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507. The campaign record shows they achieved this by recombining known methods and each other's models, including SFT on public reasoning data, RL with an agent-introduced rollout change, weight merges, and self-distillation.

局限性

  • The study addresses a weaker, measurable form of recursive self-improvement rather than full recursive self-improvement.
  • The agents operate in a sandbox and cannot use external teacher models.
