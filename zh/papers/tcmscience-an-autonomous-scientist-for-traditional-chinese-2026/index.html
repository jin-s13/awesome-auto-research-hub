深度分析

创新点

  • Separation of language-model proposals from execution and release authority
  • Architecture combining a trusted kernel, a scientific compiler, and claim-support contracts
  • Compilation of plans into typed operations with explicit execution permissions and pre-release checks for source provenance, study design, and claim scope
  • Large model-independent resource layer: 117 public-source connectors with 444 typed operations across 29 domains, 150 native tools across 12 domains, and a TCM data hub with 136 databases, 76 dataset specifications, and 84,294 formula-source records
  • Layered governance that jointly reduces release of erroneous outputs

方法

TCMScience uses a trusted kernel, scientific compiler, and claim-support contracts to compile language-model plans into typed operations, bound execution by explicit permissions, and check candidate findings against source provenance, study design, and claim scope before release. It integrates a capability registry of 117 public-source connectors with 444 typed operations across 29 domains, 150 native tools across 12 domains, and a TCM data hub cataloging 136 databases, 76 dataset specifications, and 84,294 formula-source records, supporting browser and local execution interfaces. Evaluations include a network-pharmacology analysis of Gegen Qinlian Tang and a governance ablation over 121 constructed erroneous outputs spanning 26 classes plus 10 compliant controls.

关键结果

In network-pharmacology analysis, replacing the whole-proteome background with the assayed-protein background changed significant pathways from 66 to zero, while incorporating inactive screening records identified nine pathways including drug-metabolism signals retained as mechanistic hypotheses. In the governance ablation, 121 errors were released without governance, 57 with the domain layer, 7 with the kernel, and 0 with both layers; all 10 compliant controls were retained.
