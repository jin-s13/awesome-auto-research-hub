Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces idea-level extraction and control into evolutionary coding agents via ExpEvolve.
  • Discovers and leverages high-level factors from programs that explain scoring differences among programs.
  • Characterizes theoretical conditions under which discovery with idea factors improves sample efficiency.
  • Builds a practical framework that uses extracted high-level idea factors to guide exploitation from experience and exploration when discovery plateaus.

Methodology

ExpEvolve extends evolutionary coding agents by extracting high-level idea factors from programs that explain scoring differences, then uses these factors to guide exploitation and exploration. It is evaluated on 11 open-ended mathematical tasks, with transfer experiments on 4 other LLMs and 6 machine learning tasks from MLS-Bench. The work includes theoretical analysis of sample efficiency and empirical comparisons measuring final performance and sample efficiency.

Key Results

ExpEvolve significantly improves final performance and sample efficiency by up to 5.0x across 11 open-ended mathematical tasks. Improvements also transfer to 4 other LLMs and to 6 machine learning tasks from MLS-Bench, with analysis showing better exploration and exploitation via discovered idea-level factors.
