Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces PhysClosureBench, a benchmark of 50 expert-verified, goal-specified research tasks spanning strong-field, attosecond, and neighboring physics areas.
  • Proposes a 20-criterion Atomic Score to measure local scientific accomplishment.
  • Introduces Research Closure levels RC0-RC4 to assess whether indispensable evidence requirements and applicable scope conditions are satisfied.
  • Evaluates seven frontier LLMs and reveals a gap between local scientific accomplishment and complete research closure.

Methodology

The paper introduces PhysClosureBench, a 50-task benchmark where each task specifies a research objective and essential scientific constraints while leaving the agent to organize the investigation and validate evidence. Agent outputs are scored using a 20-criterion Atomic Score for local scientific accomplishment and a Research Closure scale RC0-RC4 based on indispensable evidence requirements and applicable scope conditions. Seven frontier LLMs are evaluated across the benchmark.

Key Results

Claude-Opus-5 achieves the highest mean Atomic Score of 6.29/10 but reaches RC4 in only 32.7% of attempts. Across the benchmark, 36.2% of attempts scoring at least 8/10 still fall short of RC4, and all seven models reach RC4 on substantially fewer tasks in all three runs than in at least one.

Limitations

  • High Atomic Scores do not guarantee research closure: 36.2% of attempts scoring at least 8/10 still fall short of RC4.
  • The best model reaches RC4 in only 32.7% of attempts, and all seven models are inconsistent across runs.
  • Case-level analysis shows incomplete closure can result from misinterpretation of generated results.
  • Case-level analysis shows incomplete closure can result from unresolved physical applicability limits.
