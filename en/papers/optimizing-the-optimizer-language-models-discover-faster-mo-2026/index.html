Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Language-model-driven autoresearch that rewrites the optimizer itself to minimize force-call counts in molecular geometry optimization.
  • Two admission gates that reject premature stopping and improvements that do not generalize to unseen molecules.
  • AutoSella, a family of two optimizers derived from Sella that generalizes across held-out molecular benchmarks and potentials.
  • Demonstrated force-call reduction at the r2SCAN-3c DFT level, requiring only 40.2–77.2% of Sella's force calls without using DFT gradients during search.

Methodology

An agent performs autoresearch by rewriting the optimizer itself to minimize force-call counts, starting from Sella, the fastest open-source optimizer available. The search is restrained by two admission gates that reject premature stopping and non-generalizing improvements. Evaluation uses held-out molecular benchmarks and potentials not used during the search, with DFT validation at the r2SCAN-3c level.

Key Results

Both AutoSella optimizers deliver consistent force-call reductions relative to Sella across held-out molecular benchmarks and unseen potentials. At the r2SCAN-3c DFT level, the best variant requires only 40.2–77.2% of Sella's force calls while achieving the same energy reduction.
