Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Persistent, self-improving autonomous research agent that sustains investigations across model sessions and evolves methods through accumulated experience.
  • Combines persistent research history, hierarchical memory, branching, and independent review to maintain continuity, learn from experiments, and explore or revisit alternatives.
  • Runs experiments across session boundaries with outputs evaluated by a fixed verifier.
  • Extends the research process to algorithmic coding via Épi-turbo, a lightweight configuration that develops executable solvers.

Methodology

Épi maintains persistent research history and hierarchical memory, uses branching and independent review, and runs experiments across session boundaries with a fixed verifier evaluating outputs. Épi-turbo is evaluated on 30 FrontierOR tasks against Codex using the same underlying model and reasoning effort, comparing total benchmark score and model cost.

Key Results

Épi produced 120 record-setting results: 20 numerical bound records across 12 families and 100 construction records across eight families. It achieved an approximately 84-fold narrower interval for the n-queens constant and a more than 3.6-million-fold narrower rigorous interval for the quartic Feigenbaum attractor dimension, established via exact dual certificates that the Cohn-Elkies linear-programming bound cannot be sharp in dimensions 10 and 11, and gave a computer-assisted proof of Tuza's conjecture for split graphs with an eight-vertex clique part and at most three triangle-active neighborhood types; Épi-turbo achieved a 5.4% higher total benchmark score than Codex at 64% of model cost on 30 FrontierOR tasks.
