Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Scientist-centric, domain-agnostic agentic AI framework that combines autonomous investigation with scientist-directed hypothesis steering, allowing scientists to select, refine, or replace hypotheses between research rounds.
  • Audit agents that interrogate checkpointed records to trace claims, reproduce analyses, and inform subsequent research decisions.
  • A rigorous checkpointing system that enables highly capable audit agents as a scalable form of oversight for agentic research.

Methodology

DiscoveryAgent is evaluated on two predictive modeling tasks—variable-star classification and protein variant-effect prediction—against baselines including 106 ProteinGym baselines, and in three expert-led case studies across physical, social, and life science. The system generates checkpointed research records that audit agents interrogate to trace claims and reproduce analyses, while scientists select, refine, or replace hypotheses between research rounds. Reported metrics include classification AUROC, error-identification rates, and case-study empirical outcomes.

Key Results

DiscoveryAgent produced the best variable-star classifier among evaluated baselines and, with scientist steering, a protein variant-effect predictor surpassed by only one of 106 ProteinGym baselines. Audit agents autonomously identified errors at higher rates than previous AI scientist agents; scientists using audit agents identified 14x more valid errors than manual review; case studies achieved 0.971 AUROC for low-redshift classification, documented an 83% rise in Florida homeowners-insurance premiums from 2013 to 2023 with a regressive burden, and identified CD4+ T-cell relay genes enriched for autoimmune disease associations.
