Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Formulates collaborative scientific discovery as evidence transfer between parallel principle-evolution branches instead of sequential search.
  • Introduces COEVOLVE, which uses a coordination core over parallel branches to enable collaboration through shared measurements while keeping principle posteriors separate.
  • Integrates value-of-information-gated routing and context-discounted likelihood injection to control evidence transfer.
  • Establishes conditions under which evidence sharing accelerates parallel discovery and when transfer safeguards are needed to limit negative or inert transfers.

Methodology

COEVOLVE coordinates parallel principle-evolution branches through a central core, sharing measurements across branches while maintaining separate posteriors via value-of-information-gated routing and context-discounted likelihood injection. It is evaluated on six scientific-discovery tasks under a matched evaluation budget, compared against single-branch principle evolution on a GPT-5.6-Terra backbone, and on five auto-research tasks delegated to an autonomous research harness with published SOTA anchors as reference.

Key Results

Under a matched evaluation budget, COEVOLVE achieves 66.5% mean solution quality versus 57.0% for single-branch principle evolution, with a 1.80x mean wall-clock speedup on GPT-5.6-Terra. On five auto-research tasks, it is the only arm whose mean stays above the published SOTA anchor on every task.

Limitations

  • Without transfer safeguards, evidence sharing can produce negative or inert transfers, so safeguards are necessary.
