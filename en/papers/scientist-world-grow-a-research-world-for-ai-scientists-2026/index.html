Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Transforms research papers into executable environments via an autonomous pipeline that extracts open-ended research questions and reproduces proposed methods as baselines.
  • Introduces Scientist World as an evaluation setting where AI-Scientists are scored against what human authors achieved.
  • Releases the first 1,000 verified executable research environments.
  • Provides a large-scale research trace dataset of 6,400 hours recording AI scientists' ideas, experiments, failures, and successes.
  • Uses the collected research traces as a training ground to improve a smaller model's research ideation by +158%, matching frontier models.

Methodology

An autonomous pipeline converts papers into executable environments by extracting each paper's open-ended research question and reproducing its proposed method as the baseline. AI-Scientists entering these environments are evaluated against the results achieved by the human authors. The authors release the first 1,000 verified environments and use the resulting research traces for training smaller models.

Key Results

Frontier AI-Scientists surpass the human researchers 74% of the time in the released environments. The dataset contains 6,400 hours of research traces, and training on these traces raises a small model by +158% in research ideation, matching frontier models.
