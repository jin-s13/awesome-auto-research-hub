Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Methods-Entity Knowledge Graph (MEKG) encoding method-level follow-on, competition, and shared-resource relations instead of treating papers as flat documents
  • SurveyGraph framework using the MEKG to drive core-paper discovery, graph-aware retrieval, hybrid outlining, and evidence-gated narrative synthesis
  • Semantic evidence gate for citation grounding during generation
  • Positioning MEKG as an auditable structural layer for typed diagnostics, relation-specific filtering, and controlled survey generation

Methodology

SurveyGraph uses a Methods-Entity Knowledge Graph (MEKG) encoding method-level relations to support core-paper discovery, retrieval, hybrid outlining, and evidence-gated narrative synthesis. Evaluation includes a controlled seven-method equal-budget benchmark, an archived graph-native diagnostic against flat lexical baselines, semantic edge and citation audits, human adjudication, a SurveyBench-facing collection probe, and same-backend Qwen diagnostics. Metrics include recall, AUC, citation support, coverage, and Cohen's kappa.

Key Results

Graph-aware retrieval nearly doubled recall over flat lexical baselines across eight runs (p = 0.004), and a semantic evidence gate achieved AUC = 0.75. Audits found 80.6% anchored-or-partial citation support across 160 generated sentences and 68.5% supported-or-partial semantic edges across 108 human-adjudicated edges, with improves strongest at 80.6% and extends weakest at 52.8%; a SurveyBench-facing probe reached mean reference coverage 0.521 across four topics with an eight-paper budget.

Limitations

  • Concurrent-system comparisons provide bounded context rather than matched-backend end-to-end evidence.
  • Semantic edge support was incomplete, with only 68.5% supported-or-partial relations and extends relations weakest at 52.8%.
  • SurveyBench-facing collection coverage remained moderate at 0.521 mean reference coverage across four topics with an eight-paper budget.
