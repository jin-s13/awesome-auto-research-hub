Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • BIABench: a benchmark of 16 real-world bioimage analysis tasks reconstructed from published studies with paired raw images and ground truth
  • Dual scoring system combining outcome scores based on field-standard metrics and process scores from a vision-language model judged against expert-written rubrics
  • Coverage of eleven analysis subtasks and modalities including H&E histology and single-molecule localization microscopy, spanning 2D, 3D, and time-lapse data
  • Systematic evaluation of general-purpose and biology-specific AI agents across multiple language models with repeated runs to assess reliability

Methodology

BIABench reconstructs 16 tasks from published biological studies, retaining their scientific questions, imaging data, and ground truth. Each submission receives an outcome score by comparing output files to ground truth using field-standard metrics, and a process score in which a vision-language model judges method choice and quality control against an expert-written rubric. General-purpose and biology-specific agents were evaluated across several language models with repeated runs of every task.

Key Results

Routine two-dimensional tasks were solved well, but on some tasks that added a third dimension or a time axis no agent scored above 0.19. Biological specialization, stronger models, and detailed expert instructions did not close this gap.

Limitations

  • On some tasks with a third dimension or time axis, no agent scored above 0.19
  • Agent scores varied more between repeated runs of the same agent than between different agents
  • Without ground truth, a correct run could not be distinguished from a wrong one by its process score or time spent
  • Biological specialization, stronger models, and detailed expert instructions did not close the performance gap on difficult tasks
