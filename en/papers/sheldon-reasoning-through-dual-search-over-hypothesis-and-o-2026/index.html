Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Separately represents and jointly explores hypothesis and observation spaces, rather than coupling observation discovery to existing hypotheses.
  • Actively seeks new observations along with new hypotheses, enabling discovery of observations that current hypotheses do not explain.
  • Uses unexplained observations to expose gaps in understanding and guide generation of hypotheses beyond local refinements of existing ones.
  • Introduces SHELDON, a scientific reasoning harness inspired by the hypothetico-deductive method and dual-space models of scientific discovery.

Methodology

SHELDON is a scientific reasoning harness that maintains separate hypothesis and observation spaces and performs joint search over both. It actively gathers new observations while generating new hypotheses, using observations unexplained by current hypotheses to expose gaps and motivate new investigations. The approach is evaluated on diagnosis, abstract reasoning, and optimization benchmarks against baselines.

Key Results

SHELDON outperforms evaluated baselines by up to 26.7 points across diagnosis, abstract reasoning, and optimization benchmarks. Additional analysis shows SHELDON generates up to 4.5x more novel solutions.
