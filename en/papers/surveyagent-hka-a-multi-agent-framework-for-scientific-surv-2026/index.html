Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Proposes SurveyAgent-HKA, a multi-agent framework that decomposes scientific survey generation into sub-tasks handled by LLM-powered agents.
  • Incorporates human knowledge augmentation from published surveys and peer-review comments, rather than relying only on retrieved literature.
  • Uses multi-source paper retrieval and topic clustering to construct an initial outline.
  • Refines the initial outline using outlines from related human-written surveys.
  • Retrieves and re-ranks topic-focused papers based on the refined outline to support grounded drafting.
  • Identifies common expert issues from peer-review comments of published surveys to guide survey revisions.

Methodology

SurveyAgent-HKA is a multi-agent framework that decomposes scientific survey generation into subtasks handled by LLM-powered agents. It retrieves relevant papers from multiple sources, clusters them to identify key topics and construct an initial outline, refines that outline using related human-written survey outlines, then retrieves and re-ranks topic-focused papers for drafting. It also uses common issues found in peer-review comments from published surveys to guide revisions, and is evaluated on two domains against mainstream baselines.

Key Results

In experiments on two domains, SurveyAgent-HKA outperforms mainstream baselines in citation quality, structural consistency, and content quality, while also being efficient in time and cost.
