Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Agentic framework (La Agente Óptima) that constructs and supervises Bayesian optimization campaigns across computational and experimental systems while maintaining a persistent optimization state.
  • Separation of LLM reasoning from executed campaigns so repetitive optimization loops run consistently and the agent intervenes only when interpretation or campaign revision is needed.
  • Auditable decision-making across long-running optimization campaigns.

Methodology

La Agente Óptima is an agentic framework that separates LLM reasoning from executed Bayesian optimization campaigns and maintains a persistent optimization state. It was evaluated using ablation studies, five digital discovery tasks, and two physical platforms. Evaluations included a closed-loop contact angle optimization campaign and a five-day multi-objective flow-chemistry campaign.

Key Results

Óptima maintained executable campaigns as problems and execution environments evolved. In contact angle optimization it identified and corrected a mid-run measurement failure, bringing contact angle from 71.4 to 67.8 degrees, and inferred the target was likely unattainable with available reagents; in flow chemistry it increased yield from 30% to 59% over 23 experiments and was cheaper and more material-efficient than a human-directed campaign despite substantial inference costs.

Limitations

  • Substantial inference costs were incurred.
  • The contact angle optimization did not reach the 64-66 degree target, with Óptima inferring it was likely unattainable with available reagents.
